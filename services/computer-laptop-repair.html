Troubleshooting, servicing and upgrades

The right work depends on the fault and device. Tell us the symptoms rather than assuming that a replacement part is needed.

  • PC fault finding and diagnostic assessment
  • Software troubleshooting, performance optimisation and malware removal
  • SSD or HDD replacement, with cloning or data transfer assessed separately
  • RAM replacement or upgrades, subject to model compatibility
  • Laptop servicing, with replacement parts quoted separately
  • Windows installation where agreed, with software licensing confirmed

Know the cost before the repair is agreed

PC troubleshooting has an approved starting price. Advanced software troubleshooting and malware removal use the listed hourly rates. Customer drop-off diagnostics cost €40 standalone and the separate diagnostic fee is waived if you proceed with the repair. Standalone on-site diagnostics have a €50 minimum labour charge plus travel. RAM replacement starts from €40 labour, with replacement memory charged separately where applicable. Drives, cloning, data transfer and other replacement parts require confirmation.

Back up important files first

Make a current backup of important files before repair or upgrade work if the device allows it. Tell us if it will not start or if the only copy of important data is on it. Data preservation, cloning or recovery cannot be guaranteed. Work involving files or a failing drive must be discussed before it is agreed.

  • Drive compatibility, capacity and software licences affect the quote
  • RAM upgrades depend on the device’s supported memory and whether it is replaceable
  • Standard thermal paste is included in the eventual laptop-servicing quotation and is not charged a second time
  • Any additional work is discussed before it is agreed

Your questions answered

Is there a diagnostic charge, and is it waived if I proceed?

Customer drop-off diagnostics cost €40 standalone, waived if you proceed with the repair. Standalone on-site diagnostics have a €50 minimum labour charge plus travel. The estimator does not deduct a waiver before the repair is confirmed, and travel or replacement parts are not automatically waived. For diagnostics and repair covering the same work, the confirmed quote avoids charging twice.

Can you upgrade RAM or replace an SSD or hard drive?

We offer RAM replacement and SSD or HDD work where the device is suitable. Send the exact model so compatibility and the scope can be checked. Parts, cloning, data transfer and Windows installation require confirmation; they are not automatically included.

Will my files be safe?

Back up important files before work begins where possible, and tell us about anything that is not backed up. Data preservation or recovery cannot be guaranteed. Any file-transfer or recovery work needs an agreed scope.

Is malware removal a fixed fee?

Virus and malware removal is €50 per hour with a €50 minimum. Advanced software troubleshooting is also €50 per hour. Hourly work is not included in the automatic multi-service discount.

Is thermal paste charged separately during laptop servicing?

Standard thermal paste is included in the eventual laptop-servicing quotation. The servicing price and any replacement parts still require confirmation; the included paste is not added a second time.
