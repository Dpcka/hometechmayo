Wi-Fi is not the same as the broadband connection

Wi-Fi connects devices within your home; the internet service provider supplies the connection to the property. A weak signal, device setting or router issue may be local, while an outage or line fault may need the provider. Tell us what works and what does not so we can agree a sensible starting point. We cannot guarantee a particular speed or coverage in every room.

Equipment and work agreed separately

Let us know whether you already have a router, mesh system, extender or printer. New hardware is not included in the starting labour prices. Wider home-network setups require a custom quote rather than an assumed flat price. We do not promise that an existing extender will solve a coverage problem.

Your questions answered

Can you improve my internet provider’s connection?

We can help identify local Wi-Fi or device issues, but a provider outage or line fault may need the internet provider. We do not guarantee a broadband speed or promise to repair the provider’s network.

Should I buy a mesh system or extender before booking?

Tell us what equipment you have and where the signal fails first. The setup and equipment requirements can then be discussed. New equipment is quoted separately; no particular product or coverage result is assumed.

Can you connect a printer to the network?

Yes, printer and device setup is in the approved catalogue. The starting labour price is €40, subject to the €50 minimum labour charge per visit and any applicable travel.

Is troubleshooting a fixed-price service?

Wi-Fi troubleshooting is €50 per hour. Setup tasks have their own starting prices, and a wider home-network installation needs a custom quote. Hourly troubleshooting is excluded from automatic multi-service discounts.

Will every room have the same speed afterwards?

No particular speed or whole-home coverage is guaranteed. Building layout, wall construction, equipment, devices and the provider connection affect the result.
