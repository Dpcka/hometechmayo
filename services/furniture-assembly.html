What the assembly covers

The agreed assembly work covers putting the item together, aligning its components and checking its stability and function. Tell us where the finished furniture is to go so positioning can be included in the agreed scope.

  • Assembly-only prices are starting labour prices
  • Wall fixing and assembly plus installation require a custom quote
  • Large or complex furniture is assessed individually
  • Changes to existing furniture are quoted separately; we do not manufacture complete custom furniture

Before your appointment

Have the boxes, instructions and supplied fittings ready, and allow a clear working area near the intended position. Let us know about stairs, restricted access, missing parts or packaging that needs moving before the visit. Wall fixing depends on the furniture, wall and suitable fixings; it is agreed separately.

Materials and additional work

You can supply the required materials, ask us to supply them, or tell us you are unsure. Choose the answer for each service in the estimator. Unknown material costs are not guessed or discounted; any included consumables are not charged again.

Several services in one visit?

For qualifying fixed-price main service units, including quantities, the labour discount is 0% for 1–5 units, 5% for 6–10 and 10% for 11 or more. Minor add-ons do not increase the qualifying count. Materials, travel, hourly work, day packages and Custom Quote items are excluded. The visit minimum still applies after the discount.

Your questions answered

Can you assemble IKEA, JYSK and Wayfair furniture?

Yes. Our current services cover IKEA and JYSK furniture, and our portfolio includes a completed Wayfair console table. Send the product link or model details so the scope can be confirmed.

Is wall fixing included in the assembly price?

No. The listed furniture prices are for assembly only. Installation-only and assembly-plus-installation work require a custom quote, including confirmation of suitable walls and fixings.

Can I book several items together?

Yes. Add each item and quantity to the existing estimator. The multi-service discount applies only to qualifying fixed-price labour; hourly work, packages, minor add-ons, custom work, materials and travel are excluded.

What if parts or instructions are missing?

Let us know before the visit and include the product details. Missing or damaged components can affect whether the item can be assembled, so the work and any additional visit must be agreed.

Do you make bespoke furniture?

No. We assemble supplied furniture. Adjustments or custom fitting to existing furniture are assessed separately; complete custom furniture manufacturing is not offered.
