Wall type and bracket suitability matter

The right approach depends on the wall construction, TV model, mounting pattern and bracket. Tell us if the wall is plasterboard, masonry or another construction, if you know. We cannot assume that every wall or bracket is suitable, and we do not promise universal compatibility.

  • You may supply a suitable bracket, subject to checking
  • If we supply a bracket, its material cost is quoted separately
  • Existing sockets and cable routes affect the available finish
  • Restricted electrical work is not included

Your questions answered

Do I need to buy a bracket first?

You can supply a suitable bracket or ask us to supply one. Send the TV and bracket details before booking; bracket suitability must be checked, and a supplied bracket is priced separately from labour.

Can a TV be mounted on plasterboard?

It depends on the construction, fixing points, TV and bracket. Send photos and any information about the wall. Suitability and the fixing method must be confirmed before the work is agreed.

Are cables hidden inside the wall?

Not automatically. The approved add-on is cable management or trunking. Any different cable route or finish needs a confirmed scope; restricted electrical work is excluded.

What changes the quoted price?

TV size, wall construction, bracket requirements, cable work and the chosen installation package can affect the quote. TVs over 75 inches and all-inclusive installations require a custom quote.

Can I request mounting for more than one TV?

Yes. Add the quantity in the TV mounting category and provide the size, wall and bracket information for each installation. Tell us if the TVs need different options so the work can be confirmed correctly.
