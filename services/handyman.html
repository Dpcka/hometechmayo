Practical help around your home

Our handyman service covers the household tasks within our published service catalogue. Each job is checked for suitability before it is booked.

  • Shelves, curtain rails and poles, mirrors and small fittings, subject to the wall and fixings
  • Minor household repairs and maintenance tasks
  • Adjustments or custom fitting to existing furniture
  • Several suitable small jobs combined into an agreed visit

Choose the right way to price the work

Straightforward listed jobs may use fixed starting prices. For an agreed list of tasks, hourly work or an explicitly booked half-day or full-day package may be suitable. Packages are not automatically applied to fixed-price work, and the same task should not be charged twice.

  • General handyman work is charged by the hour, subject to the visit minimum
  • Half-day and full-day packages cover the agreed time and scope
  • Additional general handyman hours have their own hourly rate
  • Materials, fittings and travel are considered separately

Work outside this service

This is not a general building, electrical or plumbing contracting service. Gas connections and restricted electrical work are excluded. We do not offer painting, outdoor maintenance and cleaning, CCTV or video doorbells. Specialist or regulated work outside our scope must be handled by an appropriate trade.

Materials and additional work

You can supply the required materials, ask us to supply them, or tell us you are unsure. Choose the answer for each service in the estimator. Unknown material costs are not guessed or discounted; any included consumables are not charged again.

Several services in one visit?

For qualifying fixed-price main service units, including quantities, the labour discount is 0% for 1–5 units, 5% for 6–10 and 10% for 11 or more. Minor add-ons do not increase the qualifying count. Materials, travel, hourly work, day packages and Custom Quote items are excluded. The visit minimum still applies after the discount.

Your questions answered

Can several small jobs be done during one visit?

Yes, where the tasks and access are suitable. Send the full list first so we can agree the scope, time and materials. Travel is charged once per visit, not once per task.

Is one hour of handyman work charged at the hourly rate only?

There is a minimum labour charge of €50 per separate visit. The general handyman rate is €45 per hour, so a one-hour visit is subject to that minimum, plus any applicable travel or materials.

Will you supply shelves, brackets and fixings?

Tell us what you already have. If materials or fittings are needed, their suitability and cost must be confirmed separately. Unconfirmed material prices are not included in the estimator.

Do day packages receive the multi-service discount?

No. Hourly services and half-day or full-day packages are excluded. Qualifying fixed-price main service units can receive the existing labour discount; minor add-ons do not increase the qualifying count.

Can you take on electrical, gas or general plumbing work?

This page does not offer a general electrical, gas or plumbing service. Gas connections and restricted electrical work are excluded, and any task outside our published scope needs an appropriate trade.
