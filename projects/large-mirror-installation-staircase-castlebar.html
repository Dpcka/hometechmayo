Careful planning above a staircase

Restricted access above the stairs required careful planning, precise measurement and positioning of the mounting points, suitable fixings for the block wall, and a coordinated two-person approach. The mirror and surrounding interior were protected during handling and installation.

The installation was completed on 21 November 2026 in 2 hours 21 minutes. The finished mirror complements the staircase and crystal chandelier, creating a striking feature within the home’s interior.
